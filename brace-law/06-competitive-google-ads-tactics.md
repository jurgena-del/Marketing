# Brace Law — Competitive Google Ads Tactics vs. Diamond and Diamond
**Focus:** Outperform Diamond and Diamond in paid search for immigration and family law
**Prepared:** Apr 30, 2026

---

## The Competitive Landscape in Paid Search

Diamond and Diamond runs Google Ads across all their practice areas. For immigration and family law specifically, they are a generalist competing against specialists. Their ads likely:
- Use generic law firm messaging ("free consultation," "experienced lawyers")
- Send traffic to generic practice area pages, not specialized landing pages
- Have lower Quality Scores on immigration-specific terms (their site authority is in injury law)

**Brace Law's advantage:** Higher relevance = higher Quality Score = lower cost per click AND higher ad position for the same or less spend.

---

## Quality Score Strategy

Google rewards relevance. The tighter the match between keyword → ad → landing page, the higher the Quality Score (1–10). A Quality Score of 8 vs. 4 can cut cost per click by 50%.

**Diamond and Diamond's weakness:** Their immigration landing pages are sub-pages of a personal injury site. The relevance chain is broken.

**Brace Law's opportunity:**
- Build dedicated immigration and family law landing pages
- Match every ad group's keyword theme precisely to the ad copy
- Match every ad to a landing page with the same headline

Target Quality Score: **7–9 on all primary keywords**

## Campaign-by-Campaign Competitive Tactics

### Campaign 1: IMM | General | Hamilton — ATTACK MODE

This is Brace Law's home turf. Hamilton immigration searches should be dominated.

**Bid strategy:** Bid aggressively on Hamilton-specific terms. Diamond and Diamond is a Toronto-first brand — they will bid less on Hamilton-specific terms.

**Target keywords:**
```
[immigration lawyer hamilton]
[immigration lawyer hamilton ontario]
"immigration lawyer hamilton"
"immigration attorney hamilton"
"immigration law firm hamilton ontario"
```

**Ad copy — emphasize local:**
```
Headline 1: Hamilton Immigration Lawyers
Headline 2: Free Consultation — Call Today
Headline 3: Serving Hamilton Since [Year]
Desc 1: Dedicated immigration lawyers based in Hamilton. Visas, sponsorships, appeals & more.
Desc 2: Not a Toronto firm with a Hamilton office — we're Hamilton's own immigration law team.
```

**The shot at Diamond and Diamond (without naming them):**
"Not a Toronto firm with a Hamilton office" — this directly counters their model without mentioning them by name, which is against Google policy.

**Competitive position:** Diamond and Diamond likely has weak refusal-specific content. Their brand is not built on fighting IRCC decisions.

**Bid strategy:** Bid on intent-heavy terms. These clients are in crisis mode — they convert quickly.

**Ad copy:**

**"Not a side service"** — again, a direct positioning statement against firms that do immigration as an add-on.

## Defensive Tactics — Protect Brace Law's Brand

### Brand Campaign (Protect Brace Law Name)
Always run a brand campaign bidding on "Brace Law" and "bracelaw.ca" — even though competitors likely aren't bidding on it yet. Cost is near zero; ensures no competitor steals your branded traffic.

### Competitor Conquesting (Advanced)
Google Ads policy allows bidding on competitor keywords but NOT using their name in ad copy.

Consider bidding on:
```
"diamond and diamond immigration"
"diamond diamond immigration lawyer"
"diamond and diamond family law"
```

**Ad copy if conquesting:**
```
Headline 1: Looking for an Immigration Lawyer?
Headline 2: Brace Law — Immigration Specialists
Headline 3: Free Consultation — Hamilton & GTA
Desc 1: Get a firm focused 100% on immigration law — not a personal injury firm that added it.
Desc 2: Dedicated immigration and family lawyers serving Hamilton and the GTA. Book today.
```

**Important:** Never use "Diamond and Diamond" in ad copy — Google policy violation. Bidding on the keywords is allowed; using the name in ads is not.

## Bidding Strategy vs. Diamond and Diamond

Diamond and Diamond has a large ad budget. Do not try to outspend them on broad terms. Instead:

| Tactic | Description |
|---|---|
| Win on relevance | Higher Quality Score = lower CPC than their generic ads |
| Own the niche terms | Refusal appeals, spousal sponsorship, Hamilton-specific — they bid less here |
| Daypart aggressively | Focus spend on Weekdays 11AM–7PM (top conversion window per account data) |
| Geographic precision | Target specific high-immigration postal codes in GTAH |
| Mobile bid adjustment | -10% mobile (converts lower per data), +20% desktop |
| Long-tail dominance | Specific 3–4 word phrases convert better and cost less than broad terms |
