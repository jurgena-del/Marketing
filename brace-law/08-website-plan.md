## Section 1 — Technical Fixes (Do First)

1. **Fix Google Tag Manager / conversion tags immediately**
   - 2 tags inactive per Google Ads dashboard
   - Install GTM correctly on every page including thank-you/confirmation pages
   - Verify with Google Tag Assistant after installation

2. **Install Facebook Pixel** for retargeting (if not already installed)

3. **Page speed audit**
   - Run every page through Google PageSpeed Insights
   - Target: 90+ score on mobile, 95+ on desktop
   - Common fixes: compress images, enable caching, remove unused scripts, use WebP image format

4. **Mobile responsiveness audit**
   - Test every page on iPhone and Android
   - Phone number must be click-to-call on mobile on every single page
   - Forms must be thumb-friendly (large fields, large submit button)
   - Menu must be easy to navigate on small screens

5. **SSL / security** — confirm HTTPS on all pages (already likely done but verify)

6. **Fix any broken links** — run a broken link checker (Screaming Frog or Ahrefs)

7. **Set up Google Analytics 4** properly with conversion events:
   - Form submission
   - Phone call click
   - Consultation booking
   - Time on page > 3 minutes (engagement signal)

8. **Connect Google Search Console** — monitor which keywords drive organic traffic and which pages have issues

## Section 2 — Homepage Overhaul

The homepage is the single most important page. It must immediately communicate who Brace Law is, who they serve, and what to do next.

### Above the Fold (what visitors see without scrolling)

Must include ALL of the following:

- **Headline:** Clear, specific, location-based
  - Current issue: likely too generic
  - Recommended: "Hamilton & GTA's Dedicated Immigration and Family Law Firm"
- **Subheadline:** One sentence on what you do and for whom
  - "We help individuals and families navigate Canadian immigration, family law, and more — from offices in Hamilton, Vaughan, and Oakville."
- **Primary CTA button:** "Book Your Free Consultation" — large, high-contrast color
- **Secondary CTA:** Phone number — large, click-to-call
- **Trust bar:** "Award-Winning Since 2020 | 200+ 5-Star Reviews | Featured on CTV & Toronto Star"
- **Professional hero image:** Team photo or professional office photo — not stock imagery

### Below the Fold Sections (in order)

1. **Practice Areas** — clear icons/cards for:
   - Immigration Law
   - Family Law
   - Criminal Law
   - Civil Litigation
   - Real Estate Law
   - Wills & Estates
   - Each card links to its dedicated page

2. **Why Brace Law** — 4 bullet points maximum:
   - Immigration specialists, not a side service
   - Multilingual team (English, Arabic, and more)
   - Free consultations
   - Award-winning — recognized since 2020

3. **Social Proof Strip** — rotating client testimonials with 5 stars, first name, and case type

4. **About Lusi Brace** — short paragraph + professional photo:
   - Featured in CTV, Toronto Star, London Free Press
   - Award-winning principal lawyer
   - Link to full team page

5. **Awards and Media Logos** — logos of CTV, Toronto Star, Three Best Rated award, etc.

6. **Office Locations Map** — visual map showing Hamilton, Vaughan, Oakville offices with addresses

7. **Latest Blog Posts** — 3 most recent articles (feeds SEO and shows active expertise)

8. **Final CTA Section** — "Ready to get started? Book your free consultation today."
   - Large button + phone number

A blog is one of the highest-ROI investments for a law firm website. Every article captures search traffic for years.

**Setup:**
- Blog at /blog or /resources
- Minimum 2 new articles per month
- Each article 800–1,500 words
- Each article targets a specific search query

5. "How to bring your parents to Canada in 2026"
6. "Work permit to permanent residence — the roadmap"
7. "PGWP expired — what are your options?"
8. "Hamilton immigration lawyers — how to choose the right one"

10. "Divorce in Canada when one partner is not a citizen or PR"
11. "Child custody in Ontario — what courts look at"

**Article structure (every article):**
- H1: target keyword
- Introduction: answer the question immediately

- Author byline: Lusi Brace or the relevant lawyer

## Section 8 — Website Analytics and Tracking Setup

Without tracking, you cannot measure growth. Set all of this up in Month 1.

1. Google Analytics 4 — track all pages, all sessions, all conversion events
2. Google Search Console — monitor keyword rankings and page errors
3. Google Ads conversion tags — fix the 2 broken tags immediately
4. Facebook Pixel — for retargeting ads
5. Call tracking — use a tool like CallRail to assign unique phone numbers to:
   - Google Ads (know exactly which campaign drove the call)
   - Organic search
   - Social media
   - Direct traffic
6. Heatmap tool — Hotjar or Microsoft Clarity (free):
   - See where users click, scroll, and drop off
   - Identify which pages are losing visitors before the CTA
7. Monthly reporting dashboard — Google Looker Studio:
   - Sessions, leads, conversion rate, cost per lead, by channel
