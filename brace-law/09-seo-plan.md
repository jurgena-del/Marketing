## Section 1 — Technical SEO (Month 1 — Non-Negotiable)

Complete all of these before any content work. Technical problems cap your rankings regardless of content quality.

1. **Crawl the entire site** with Screaming Frog (free up to 500 URLs) or Ahrefs
   - Find: broken links (404s), redirect chains, duplicate content, missing meta tags
   - Fix every issue found

2. **Title tags** — every page needs a unique, keyword-rich title tag (50–60 characters):
   - Format: Primary Keyword | Secondary Keyword | Brace Law
   - Example: "Immigration Lawyer Hamilton | Free Consultation | Brace Law"
   - No two pages should have the same title tag

3. **Meta descriptions** — every page needs a unique meta description (150–160 characters):
   - Include the primary keyword, a value proposition, and a CTA
   - Example: "Brace Law's Hamilton immigration lawyers handle visas, sponsorships, appeals & more. Award-winning firm. Book your free consultation today."

4. **H1 tags** — one H1 per page, containing the primary keyword

5. **Image alt text** — every image must have descriptive alt text including keywords where natural

6. **Canonical tags** — set canonical tags to prevent duplicate content issues across location pages

7. **XML sitemap** — submit to Google Search Console; update whenever new pages are added

8. **Robots.txt** — ensure no important pages are accidentally blocked

9. **Schema markup** — implement on all key pages:
   - LegalService schema on all practice area pages
   - LocalBusiness schema on all location pages (with address, phone, hours)
   - FAQ schema on all pages with FAQ sections
   - Person schema for Lusi Brace's profile page
   - Review/AggregateRating schema on homepage and location pages

10. **Core Web Vitals** — pass all three:
    - Largest Contentful Paint (LCP): under 2.5 seconds
    - First Input Delay (FID): under 100ms
    - Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS): under 0.1
    - Fix via: image compression, lazy loading, remove render-blocking scripts

11. **HTTPS** — confirm all pages are secure; fix any mixed content warnings

12. **Mobile-first indexing** — Google indexes the mobile version; test every page on mobile

## Section 5 — Link Building (Off-Page SEO)

Links from other websites to bracelaw.ca increase the site's authority and rankings.

### Link Building Tactics for a Law Firm

1. **Local media PR** — every time Lusi Brace is quoted in CTV, Toronto Star, or any publication, that publication links to bracelaw.ca. Actively pitch stories to local media (see PR plan).

2. **Settlement agency partnerships** — ask community organizations to link to Brace Law's resource pages from their "legal resources" sections

3. **Guest posts on legal blogs** — write articles for Canadian legal publications:
   - Canadian Lawyer Magazine
   - Law Times
   - Slaw.ca (Canadian legal blog)
   - These links are high authority

4. **Community sponsorships** — sponsor local Hamilton or Vaughan events; sponsors typically get a website link

5. **IRCC news commentary** — when IRCC releases major policy changes, publish a blog post quickly and pitch it to immigration news sites for coverage

6. **Legal directories** — all the citation directories above also provide backlinks

7. **YouTube channel** — create a YouTube channel and embed videos in blog posts; YouTube is owned by Google and video embeds add link value

8. **Podcast guest appearances** — appear on immigration, newcomer, or Ontario business podcasts; hosts typically link to guest websites in show notes

### Link Building — What NOT to Do

- Do not buy links from link farms or low-quality directories
- Do not participate in link exchanges ("I'll link to you if you link to me")
- Do not use automated link building tools
- One high-quality link from CBC, Toronto Star, or Law Society beats 100 low-quality links

## Section 6 — AI Search Visibility (2026 Priority)

In 2026, a significant portion of legal searches now go through AI assistants — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overview, Gemini. If Brace Law is not mentioned in AI responses, it is losing visibility.

**How to appear in AI search results:**

1. **Structured content** — AI tools pull from clearly structured pages. Use H2/H3 headers, bullet lists, FAQ sections. Pages that look like reference guides get cited.

2. **FAQ schema** — implement on every page; AI tools pull directly from FAQ schema

3. **Be quoted in authoritative sources** — AI tools heavily cite Wikipedia, government sites, and authoritative news. Get Lusi Brace quoted in Toronto Star, CBC, or Law Times — AI tools will start associating Brace Law with immigration expertise.

4. **Wikipedia / Wikidata** — not easily created for a law firm but being mentioned in Ontario legal community resources helps

5. **Answer questions directly** — write blog posts that directly answer common questions:
   - "What is the best immigration lawyer in Hamilton?" → Write an FAQ page answering this with Brace Law's credentials
   - "How do I appeal an immigration refusal in Canada?" → Deep guide on /immigration-refusal-appeal

6. **Google AI Overviews** — these pull from the top-ranked organic pages. Ranking #1–3 organically means appearing in the AI Overview box at the top of Google results — double the visibility.
